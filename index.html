What is ROT13?
ROT13 shifts each letter by 13 places.

A ↔ NB ↔ OC ↔ PD ↔ Q E ↔ RF ↔ SG ↔ TH ↔ U I ↔ VJ ↔ WK ↔ XL ↔ Y M ↔ Z
Example: QHC → DUP
Replace each letter with the letter 13 positions away.
Tip: ROT13 is reversible. Applying ROT13 twice returns the original text.
